## Shopping list for the hotel visit

**Essential (about 30 photos)**
1. Exterior front, daytime (1–2)
2. Reception (1)
3. Each room category: whole room, second angle, bathroom (3 each)
4. Each apartment type: living area, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom (3–4 each)
5. One hero-quality interior, wide, with empty space on one side
6. One welcoming scene for the experience section

**If the facility exists:** lounge, restaurant or dining area, parking or security, laundry, pool, gym.

**Tips:** shoot at a consistent time of day, turn on all lamps, straighten bedding and cushions, shoot at chest height, keep verticals straight, and capture both landscape and portrait of the same spaces.

**Still needed from the hotel (not photos):** logo files, room categories and names, capacity and bed types, prices, address, phone, email, WhatsApp, check-in and check-out times, cancellation policy, confirmed amenity list.
